BMW Berlin Marathon

The BMW BERLIN-MARATHON 2026 takes place on 27 September 2026. The marathon covers 42.195 kilometres through Berlin and is considered one of the fastest marathon courses in the world. Start and finish are on Straße des 17. Juni near the Brandenburg Gate.

Lottery closed; alternative entries may be available through charity, tour operators, running camps and other guaranteed-entry routes.

Verify final race details on the official organizer website before booking or registering.

Course profile

Berlin is an extremely fast, broad and flat asphalt course with very high personal-best potential. It runs through central Berlin and combines elite performance conditions with a large international city-marathon atmosphere.

Public transport
The organizer recommends travelling to the start/finish area by public transport. Nearby stops include U Bundestag and Berlin Hauptbahnhof. The race bib is listed as valid for public transport in fare zone ABC from 24 to 27 September 2026; details are expected with the Start Card.
Parking
Public parking is not recommended. No parking is listed for the Expo at Tempelhof, and race-weekend road closures and traffic disruption are expected.
Accommodation
Book accommodation early. Practical areas include Mitte, Tiergarten, the main station area, Potsdamer Platz or locations with direct U-Bahn/S-Bahn access to the start area.
Travel notes
Use U-Bahn and S-Bahn on race day. Expect road closures, crowding around the start/finish zone and longer walking routes near security areas.
Weather
Late September in Berlin can range from cool autumn conditions to late-summer warmth.

FAQ

FAQ

When is the Berlin Marathon 2026?
The BMW BERLIN-MARATHON 2026 takes place on Sunday, 27 September 2026.
Where are the start and finish?
Start and finish are on Straße des 17. Juni, between Brandenburg Gate and Kleiner Stern.
When do the runners start?
Runners start from 09:15 in four waves: 09:15, 09:45, 10:10 and 10:40.
What is the cutoff time?
The cutoff is 6:15 hours from crossing the start line. Runners must also reach km 33 by 15:50 and km 38 by 16:35.
Is Berlin good for a personal best?
Yes. Berlin is officially described as the flattest course among the Abbott World Marathon Majors and has a long history of extremely fast performances.
Are pacers available?
Yes. Pacers are listed in all four start waves, with target times from 3:00 to 5:00 hours depending on the wave.
How does registration work?
Regular entry is handled through a lottery. For 2026, the lottery window ran from late September to early November 2025. After the lottery, alternative routes may include charity entries, tour operators and running camps.
Can the race entry be transferred?
No. The official registration information states that entries are personal and cannot be transferred to another person or another year.
Can I get a refund if I cannot race?
The official policy states that the participation fee is not refunded. The organizer recommends race cancellation insurance.
Where do runners pick up race materials?
Race materials must be collected personally at the Marathon Expo with Start Card and photo ID. Pickup by another person is not possible.
