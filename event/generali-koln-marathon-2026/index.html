The start in Deutz and finish near the cathedral are both well connected by public transport.
Parking
Public transport is recommended because start/finish areas and bridge crossings may be affected by road closures.
Accommodation
Useful areas include Innenstadt, Deutz, Altstadt and districts with direct Stadtbahn or S-Bahn access.
Travel notes
Plan for crowds around Deutz, the Rhine bridges and the cathedral finish area.
Weather
Early October in Cologne is usually mild autumn weather, with possible rain and variable wind.
