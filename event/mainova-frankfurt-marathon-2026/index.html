Public transport
The Messe/Festhalle area is close to Frankfurt Hauptbahnhof and is normally practical by S-Bahn, U-Bahn or tram.
Parking
Use public transport where possible; race-weekend road closures and Messe traffic can make driving inefficient.
Accommodation
Useful areas include Messe, Hauptbahnhof, Westend, Innenstadt and locations with direct S-Bahn/U-Bahn access.
Travel notes
Plan extra time around Messe, Friedrich-Ebert-Anlage and Festhalle because roads around the race area may be restricted.
Weather
Late October in Frankfurt is usually autumnal: often cool, sometimes wet or windy, with conditions that can be good for marathon racing if temperatures stay low.
